*Content strategy / Growth agency serving e-commerce operators*

## What is a prompt-space map?

> **Short answer:** A prompt-space map is a list of the questions a target buyer asks AI assistants on the way to a purchase, sorted by decision stage. Each prompt is matched to the page that should answer it and the proof that makes that page worth citing. It replaces a keyword list as the starting point for content in AI search.

## Who is the buyer?

An owner-operator running an established online store, typically $30K to $150K a month in revenue with an average order of $500 or more, selling functional parts rather than decor or basics. They have been burned by agencies, distrust screenshots, and increasingly check a decision with an AI assistant before booking a call.

That last habit is why the map matters. If the assistant answers their question with a competitor's page, the call never happens.

## How the map is built

1. **Start from the sales path, not a keyword tool.** The agency's funnel already follows how this buyer decides: diagnose the problem, qualify the fit, see proof, then decide. The prompts are sorted into those four stages.
2. **Write prompts the way the buyer talks.** First person, specific, a little skeptical. "Is my Meta ROAS real?" not "ROAS accuracy".
3. **Match each prompt to proof already on hand.** Answer engines cite pages with specific, checkable evidence. Prompts with existing case-study data get built first.
4. **Flag the gaps.** Where no proof exists yet, the plan names what evidence has to be created before the page is worth publishing.

## The map: 16 prompts, four stages

| Stage | Buyer prompt | Answer asset | Format | Proof on hand |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Diagnose | Why is my store getting traffic but not sales? | Traffic problem or system problem? A diagnostic explainer | Article + FAQ | CRO tests: three different constraints on one store |
| Diagnose | Why do shoppers add to cart and then leave? | What happens after Add to Cart | Test writeup | Cart redirect: ≈76% more revenue per visitor |
| Diagnose | Are my product pages losing mobile buyers? | Mobile-first product pages, explained | Article + table | Mobile rebuild: 2.2% → 4.1% conversion |
| Diagnose | Is my Meta ROAS real? | What Meta ROAS actually proves ([Sample 04](04-what-meta-roas-proves.md)) | Reference page | Six-month account: verified vs not shown |
| Qualify | Do I need a marketing agency or a better system? | Vendor vs owner: renting results or owning a system | Opinion + checklist | Point of view only. Needs a client story |
| Qualify | What should an e-commerce growth audit include? | Anatomy of a growth audit: messaging, acquisition, follow-up | Guide + HowTo | Audit structure exists. Needs a sample deliverable |
| Qualify | Is a paid audit better than a free one? | Why a diagnosis is worth paying for | Article + FAQ | Point of view only |
| Qualify | Is my store too small for a growth agency? | Who this is for, and who it is not for | Qualifier page | ICP criteria already written |
| Prove | Does Advantage+ Shopping work for catalog stores? | One account, three campaign jobs | Case study + table | 9.89x reported, 84.5% of sales from 74.3% of spend |
| Prove | Do retargeting ads work for high-ticket parts? | Retargeting as a follow-up layer | Case study | 6.20x reported, highest average order value |
| Prove | How do I improve ROAS without raising budget? | Same event, less spend ([Sample 02](02-before-after-restructure.md)) | Case study | 17.2x → about 35.9x on 2.6% less spend |
| Prove | Does social proof increase conversion? | Video, ratings, or both? A four-way test | Test writeup | 4.0% → 5.1% with both stacked |
| Decide | What should I ask a growth agency before hiring? | Twelve questions that expose cherry-picked results | Checklist + FAQ | Draws on the measurement rules in Samples 01 and 04 |
| Decide | What does a 90-day growth roadmap look like? | The 13-week build, week by week | Timeline + HowTo | Process already defined |
| Decide | How long until a new marketing system shows results? | What changes in weeks 1–13, and what doesn't | Article + FAQ | Needs dated client milestones |
| Decide | How do I compare two agency proposals? | A side-by-side scoring sheet | Template + table | No proof needed. Utility asset |

**Read:** 11 of 16 prompts can be answered now with evidence the agency already owns. Five need a client story, a sample deliverable, or dated milestones first.

## Build order

| Wave | What ships | Why first |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Wave 1 | The four Prove pages and "Is my Meta ROAS real?" | Original, specific data is what answer engines cite. These pages have it today. |
| Wave 2 | Remaining Diagnose pages and the Decide checklist and roadmap | They link down to the Wave 1 proof, which strengthens both. |
| Wave 3 | The five gap pages | Ship once the missing client story, sample audit, or milestones exist. |

## How to check who AI assistants cite today

Run every prompt in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, and Gemini before writing, then again monthly. Log the result in the same sheet so changes are visible over time.

## Rules every page in the plan follows

- Open with a direct answer of 40 to 60 words that stands alone.
- Use the buyer's question as the heading, word for word where possible.
- Put every result next to its source and what it does not prove.
- Use tables for comparisons and numbered lists for processes.
- Add Article, FAQPage, or HowTo schema, plus Person schema for the author.
- Keep the agency's name, offer, and proof figures identical on every page, profile, and directory listing.
