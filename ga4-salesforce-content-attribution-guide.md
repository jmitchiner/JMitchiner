# GA4 + Salesforce Content Attribution Guide

*This guide details my methodology for connecting top-of-funnel content marketing efforts to bottom-of-funnel revenue. It bridges the gap between marketing metrics (traffic, pageviews) and sales metrics (pipeline, closed-won deals).*

---

## The Challenge

Content marketing often struggles to prove ROI because standard analytics (like GA4) track *sessions*, while CRMs (like Salesforce) track *people* and *revenue*. This framework connects the two.

## Phase 1: GA4 Configuration for Content

1.  **Event Tracking:** Move beyond standard pageviews. Configure custom events for:
    *   `content_download` (Whitepapers, eBooks)
    *   `newsletter_signup`
    *   `demo_request_from_blog`
    *   `scroll_depth_75` (To measure actual engagement, not just clicks)
2.  **UTM Parameter Standardization:** Enforce a strict UTM tagging taxonomy for all content distributed via social, email, and paid channels.
    *   *Example:* `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=q3_supply_chain_report`
3.  **Content Grouping:** Group URLs by topic cluster or funnel stage in GA4 to analyze performance by category, rather than just individual URLs.

## Phase 2: The Salesforce Handoff

1.  **Hidden Form Fields:** Ensure all lead capture forms (HubSpot, Marketo, etc.) capture UTM parameters and the referring URL in hidden fields.
2.  **Lead Source vs. Lead Source Detail:**
    *   `Lead Source`: Broad category (e.g., "Inbound Organic", "Social Media").
    *   `Lead Source Detail`: Specific asset (e.g., "Blog: 5 Ways to Improve Visibility").
3.  **Campaign Influence:** Utilize Salesforce Campaigns to track multi-touch attribution.
    *   *First Touch:* Which piece of content brought them to the site initially?
    *   *Last Touch:* Which piece of content did they consume immediately before requesting a demo?

## Phase 3: Reporting & Pipeline Velocity

1.  **The MQL to SQL Conversion Rate:** Track how leads generated by content convert into Sales Qualified Leads compared to other channels.
2.  **Content-Influenced Pipeline:** Measure the total dollar value of open opportunities where a contact consumed content during the sales cycle.
3.  **Time-to-Close:** Analyze whether prospects who engage with content close faster than those who do not.

## Example Dashboard Metrics

When I build a content performance dashboard, these are the metrics I report to the executive team:

| Metric | Source | What It Tells Us |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Organic Traffic to Blog** | GA4 | Top-of-funnel brand awareness and SEO health. |
| **Blog-to-Lead Conversion Rate** | GA4 / CRM | How effectively content captures intent. |
| **Content-Sourced MQLs** | Salesforce | The volume of marketing-qualified leads generated directly by content. |
| **Content-Influenced Pipeline ($)** | Salesforce | The revenue value of deals where content played a role in the buyer's journey. |
