## 📈 Case Studies & Key Results

### 1. Narrative Redesign & Conversion Lift (Spiffy)
*   **The Challenge:** The existing content was generating traffic but failing to convert readers into qualified leads.
*   **The Strategy:** Implemented a comprehensive narrative redesign, aligning content with specific funnel stages and optimizing landing page message-match.
*   **The Result:** Achieved a **+35% conversion lift** within the first quarter of implementation.

### 2. AEO & Organic Reach Expansion (Riley Power Group)
*   **The Challenge:** The brand needed to establish authority in a highly technical, niche market and capture emerging AI-driven search traffic.
*   **The Strategy:** Executed an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) strategy, restructuring content to directly answer core industry queries and improving technical site architecture.
*   **The Result:** Drove a **+45% increase in organic reach** and secured multiple featured snippets.

### 3. Demand Generation Engine (TransmetriQ)
*   **The Challenge:** The sales team needed a higher volume of qualified inbound leads.
*   **The Strategy:** Built a targeted demand generation engine combining SEO-focused blog content with high-converting LinkedIn campaigns.
*   **The Result:** Delivered a **+30% increase in qualified leads** and improved the overall conversion rate by 20% over six months.
